The Backrooms


The Backrooms was a concept that originated in a 4Chan post back in the year 2019. From what I can remember, the post read as "If you aren't careful enough, you can noclip out of reality into the Backrooms". Along with the image that is shown off to the side right now. At that time the Backrooms had no set story and was just a concept, most people invisioned it as an infinite empty office space with the yellow color scheme, buzzing flourescent lighting, and the iconic chevron wallpaper.

Later, in the year 2022, a 16 year old named Kane Parsons would create a found footage horror styled video of the Backrooms and it went viral. The video was a kind of horror most people at the time didn't even convieve of and Kane Parsons would continue to make videos on the Backrooms with it being a series on his Youtube channel.

Even Later, in the year 2024, Kane Parsons was approached by A24 films and they were interested in making a Backrooms movie with him being the director. He accepted the request and they started working on the movie. The movie would release in the year 2026 and it was a big hit with Kane Parsons being the youngest director for a movie since the movie Jaws.

The Backrooms never had any set story to it. It was simply just a concept that many people had their own takes for a story on. Tons of other people before and after Kane Parsons had their own stories for what the Backrooms was and Kane's idea of it just became the most popular one and influenced many other people's ideas of the Backrooms. The other known version of the Backrooms that came before Kane Parsons Backrooms seires was the level Backrooms which was more like a gamified version of it where there are multiple different levels to the Backrooms and each lever has different entities and creatures.

One thing that heavily influenced the Backrooms as a whole was a new aesthetic that came out in 2019 which was Liminal spaces. By definition, Liminal spaces are transitionary spaces such as airports, highways or roads, hallways, trainstations, malls, and etc. but usually these places would have no people in them giving them a strange feeling because usually those places are filled with people. But just like the Backrooms the whole concept of Liminal spaces has eveolved over time into places that aren't just transitionary. But they still do hold that idea of having no people in them. The Backrooms and the idea of Liminal spaces fit together perfectly and without the idea of Liminal spaces the Backrooms would have likely never gotten as popular as it is now.

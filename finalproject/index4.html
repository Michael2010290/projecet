Half Life 2 Things

This page is dedicated to things about the game Half Life 2.


Half-Life 2 was created by Valve and is the sequel to Half-Life, Half-Life 2 started development in the June of 1999 which was six months after the realease of Half-Life. Half-Life 2 would eventually release on November 16, 2004. Half-Life 2's development period was a long and chaotic one, with the game being completly different from 1999 to around 2002 where Valve went for a much darker and grittier artstyle and story for the game than we got in 2004.

During the development period during September, a German hacker, Axel Gembe, would end up obtaining the Half-Life 2 source code and shared it with a friend who eventually leaked the code online in early October and fans ended up compiling a playable version of the game which was shown to be very unfinished for the amount of time Valve has been working on it. This leak destroyed Valve's morale and slowed development. Valve would team up with the FBI and have Axel Gembe arrested in the U.S. however he was arrested in Germany and charged with two years probation.

Half-Life 2 was developed using the Source engine which was a modified version of Half-Life's Goldsrc engine which was also a modified version of the Quake engine. They also integrated the Havok physics engine which simulated real world physics which impressed the public and made Half-Life 2 known for its impressive physics for the time. The Source engine would later be used in Valve's other games such as Counter-Strike: Source, Portal, Team Fortress 2, and Left 4 Dead.
